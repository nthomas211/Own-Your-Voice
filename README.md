## Own Your Voice is an accessible interview platform that uses speech-to-text and text-to-speech to help nonspeaking job applicants communicate more naturally, independently, and effectively during professional interviews.

# Data Sources

Training data is not stored directly in this repository.

Potential/public research sources include:

- ASL Citizen
- WLASL
- Google ASL Signs competition dataset
- Boston University ASL SignBank resources

See `docs/asl_research/dataset_sources.docx` for research notes.
